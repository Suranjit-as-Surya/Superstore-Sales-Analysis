Global Superstore Profitability Analysis (Power BI)
📌 Project Overview
As a Data Analyst, I conducted an end-to-end analysis of the Global Superstore dataset (approximately 10,000 rows of retail data) to identify why the company's net profit was significantly lower than its total revenue.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Power BI Desktop: For data modeling and visualization.

Excel/CSV: Source data format.

Power Query: For data cleaning and changing data types (e.g., converting Order_Date from Text to Date).

📊 Dashboard Features
KPI Cards: Real-time tracking of Total Revenue ($1.53M) and Net Profit ($199.55K).

Geographic Mapping: A visual breakdown of sales by state to pinpoint underperforming locations.

Profitability Bar Chart: Analyzes profit by product sub-category to find "loss makers".

Interactive Date Slicer: Allows users to filter the entire dashboard by specific years and months.

💡 Key Business Insights
The Product Drain: While sales for Tables and Bookcases are high, they are the primary drivers of negative profit.

Regional Opportunity: California remains the highest performer, whereas Texas shows high volume but negative profitability.
